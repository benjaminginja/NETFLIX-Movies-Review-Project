# Netflix Movies & TV Shows Dashboard Report
# Project Overview
Download the attached report for full insights.
This project focuses on creating a comprehensive data dashboard that visualizes key metrics related to Netflix's movie and TV show catalog from 2008 to 2020. The dashboard was built using Tableau as the data visualization tool to showcase trends and patterns within Netflix's content library. Key metrics such as content distribution by genre, ratings, release year, and country were analyzed to provide insights into Netflix’s content strategy and user preferences.
# Key Features
- Movies vs. TV Shows Distribution: A comparison of the number of movies and TV shows available on Netflix, illustrating that movies make up a larger portion of Netflix's catalog (68.42%) compared to TV shows (31.58%).

- Top Genres: Analysis of the top 10 movie genres, highlighting popular categories such as documentaries, stand-up comedy, and dramas, providing insights into viewer preferences.

- Rating Distribution: A breakdown of content based on rating categories, with a significant portion of content aimed at mature audiences (TV-MA), followed by other ratings such as TV-14 and TV-PG.

- Content by Release Year: Visualization of the total number of movies and TV shows added each year, with a notable surge in content additions between 2015 and 2019.

- Content by Country: A world map showing the distribution of Netflix content by country, with the U.S. leading in the number of titles, followed by other countries.

# Tools and Technologies Used
Data Visualization: Tableau
Data source- Netflix dataset containing information on movies and TV shows including genres, ratings, release dates, and country of origin.

# Recommendations
-Increase in TV Show Offerings: As TV shows tend to keep viewers engaged over a longer period, Netflix may want to increase its focus on original TV series to balance out the dominance of movies in its library.

Targeted Content for Younger Audiences: Given the overwhelming share of content targeted at mature viewers (TV-MA), Netflix could diversify its user base by increasing content aimed at younger audiences (PG, TV-G).

-Regional Content Expansion: While the U.S. leads in content contributions, Netflix should continue to invest in region-specific content, particularly in emerging markets, to capture more diverse global audiences.

-Content Quality Focus: As Netflix adds more content each year, maintaining a balance between quantity and quality will be crucial to retaining and growing its subscriber base.

# Conclusion
This dashboard serves as a powerful tool for understanding Netflix's content distribution, user preferences, and growth strategy. The project emphasizes the importance of data visualization in making sense of large datasets and deriving actionable insights for business decisions.
